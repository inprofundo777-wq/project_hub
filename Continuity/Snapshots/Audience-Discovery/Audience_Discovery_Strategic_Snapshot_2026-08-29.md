## 4. What Is Now Supported by Evidence

### 4.1 In Profundo has a meaningful existing corpus

The current problem cannot be reduced to:

> сначала нужно просто написать достаточно материалов.

The Blogger corpus already contains enough material to reveal recurring reader territories and meaningful relationships between articles.

---

### 4.2 Public navigation should not mirror editorial taxonomy

Working principle:

**Reader Theme ≠ Blogger Label**

and:

**Series ≠ Reader Theme**

Public architecture is more useful when it begins with:

> from which human question or situation can the reader recognize that something here may concern them?

rather than:

> how does the editorial system classify this material?

---

### 4.3 Two reader domains currently have sufficient corpus support

#### `Когда вера стала трудной`

A strong existing corpus territory.

Nested route:

`Когда не видно Божьей работы`

is sufficiently coherent to function as a reader route inside the broader domain.

#### `Вера в обычной жизни`

A second meaningful corpus center connecting faith with ordinary work, home, rest and daily life.

---

### 4.4 `Адвент и Рождество` works differently

It is better understood as a:

**seasonal route / hub**

rather than an equal permanent reader domain.

---

### 4.5 Several possible territories remain emerging

Current evidence does not yet justify promoting these into independent public domains:

- `Молитва / Писание / духовная практика`;
- `Покой внутри нагрузки`;
- `Отношения и община`;
- `Духовный рост и зрелость`.

They remain useful content-intelligence signals.

## 5. Important Corpus Signals

### SATURATION / HIGH THRESHOLD

`Когда вера стала трудной`

and especially:

`Когда не видно Божьей работы`

already contain substantial coverage.

This does not forbid further articles.

It means similar new topics should clear a higher threshold of:

- meaningful difference;
- uncovered reader need;
- new theological or experiential movement.

---

### GROWING

`Вера в обычной жизни`

appears to be a strong growing center.

---

### EMERGING

Possible future territories:

- `Покой внутри нагрузки`;
- `Молитва / Писание / духовная практика`.

They should develop through corpus evidence rather than being created prematurely as navigation categories.

---

### WEAK / UNDERCOVERED TERRITORIES

Current corpus analysis surfaced weaker coverage around:

- relationships;
- community and belonging;
- how to remain beside another person;
- responsibility and leadership;
- family and image of God;
- migration;
- life with the church after disappointment.

These are **signals, not publication quotas**.

Coverage should act as a constraint detector, not as an automatic scheduler.

## 7. What Remains Unproven

The work completed so far does **not** prove that:

- Discovery is definitively the primary constraint of In Profundo;
- current channels are the optimal acquisition channels;
- strangers who encounter In Profundo will perceive a sufficiently distinctive value proposition;
- reader navigation itself will materially improve engagement;
- current reader domains are the final public information architecture;
- weak traffic means weak content;
- good navigation will solve lack of exposure;
- search demand will translate into meaningful readership;
- In Profundo has a repeatable audience-acquisition mechanism.

These remain open empirical questions.

---

## 8. Critical Distinction

Two different problems must remain separated:

### Problem A — Exposure

**People do not encounter In Profundo.**

### Problem B — Value / Selection

**People encounter In Profundo but do not choose, continue or return.**

Current evidence is insufficient to confidently determine the relative weight of A and B.

This distinction should constrain future Audience / Discovery experiments.

---

## 9. Strategic Horizon

The next strategic horizon is broader than Blogger navigation.

The emerging question is:

> Как In Profundo получает meaningful exposure среди релевантных незнакомых людей — достаточно, чтобы начать отличать Discovery problem от Value / Selection problem?

Possible future work may therefore concern:

- external discovery;
- acquisition experiments;
- search exposure;
- platform-native discovery;
- entry assets;
- unfamiliar-reader response;
- value-proposition validation.

These are **horizon possibilities, not adopted tasks**.

The current Navigation Pilot should continue independently while this broader question is explored.

---

## 10. What Should Not Happen Next

This Snapshot is not evidence for immediately:

- redesigning Blogger;
- creating a complete information architecture;
- rebuilding Labels;
- creating more reader domains;
- expanding Series navigation;
- rewriting the existing corpus;
- mass internal linking;
- redesigning Distribution;
- declaring Discovery the proven primary bottleneck;
- launching several acquisition channels simultaneously;
- turning every corpus gap into a publication requirement.

The next development should remain proportional to evidence.

---

## 11. Re-entry Question

When Audience / Discovery returns to active strategic development, begin with:

> What is the smallest useful experiment that can give In Profundo meaningful exposure among relevant unfamiliar people and help distinguish lack of discovery from lack of perceived value?

Do not begin by asking:

> Which new channel should In Profundo launch?

Channel choice comes after the experimental question.

---

## 12. Re-entry Triggers

Re-enter this direction strategically when one or more of the following occurs:

1. Blogger Navigation Pilot accumulates meaningful evidence;
2. GSC / Blogger analytics produce interpretable external exposure;
3. an external channel begins producing unfamiliar readers;
4. a repeatable organic discovery pattern appears;
5. a controlled acquisition experiment becomes practical;
6. evidence suggests that people encounter In Profundo but systematically do not continue;
7. Owner intentionally opens the broader Discovery / Acquisition question.

Navigation Pilot alone does not need to close before broader Discovery exploration begins.
